Welcome to Our Laundry for a Cause!

This service helps people in need by letting them handle your laundry at a lower cost for you and providing them with meaningful income.

How It Works

You can weigh your laundry, get a clear price, pay in cash, and receive updates through the app when your laundry is ready.

About Our Service

This service is provided by youth in need over the age of 18, who receive most of the profit.
